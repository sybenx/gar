# Garble (GAR) and Google Assistant: parity plan

The app is called Garble since 2026-11. GAR stays the project's code name: the `gar/` modules,
the `org.stypox.dicio.gar` package, the `// GAR` markers and the repository.

Researched 2026-10-27: what Google Assistant (and Gemini's phone controls) do on an Android phone, what GAR does today, and what can work on GrapheneOS without root. Priority 1 matters most for everyday use. Status and the batch order drive phase 2; each batch updates this file as it lands.

Feasible: `offline` works without the internet; `online` follows the online-skills rule (off until the user says yes once); `needs-system` needs privileges GAR can't get (GAR opens the settings panel instead); `not-feasible` can't be done.

## Batches

**1. A clock you can trust**: Timers that keep running and ring with GAR closed, Timer extras: pause, resume, add time, show timers, stop a ringing timer, Stop or snooze a ringing alarm by voice, Stop, cancel, never mind, Change an alarm or remove all alarms.

  A timer that doesn't ring is the worst miss an assistant can have, and Android 14+'s freezer likely stalls Dicio's in-process timer. 'Stop' is what people say most around a ringing phone. All offline, no prompt change, and the stopwatch already shows the service pattern.

  Status 2026-11-01: timers moved to the clock app after a week of use (AlarmClock intents; pause, resume and add time now open the clock app's timers, since no intent does them). Status 2026-10-27: done, sentence-only, no prompt change (DESIGN.md: 'Timers', 'Alarm: ringing, change, remove all', 'Conversation', 'Stop: who takes it'). Still missing: hotword-free 'stop' and 'snooze' (GAR only hears 'stop' through the hotword or the assist gesture), the model's alarm_cancel(time: 'all') (a router change), and a check of all of it on the Pixel. ('Stop' pausing media came with batch 4.)

**2. Everyday offline answers**: Battery level and charging, Time in another place and time differences, Unit conversion, Small talk and help, Coin, dice, random number, Spelling, Repeat that, Countdowns and holidays, Math.

  High-frequency quick answers, mostly S effort, all offline. Sentence-only or Kotlin rewrites of existing tools (current_time, date_query, calculate), so the model's prompt stays as it is. Fixes a wrong answer today (time in Tokyo).

  Status 2026-10-27: done except math's '+' through the model (DESIGN.md: 'Everyday offline answers', and 'Conversation' for 'Repeat that'). Eight English-only skills (battery, world_time, units, math, random, spelling, holiday, small_talk) plus Kotlin answers for the model's current_time, calculate and date_query calls; no tool, prompt or grammar change. Still missing: lunar and non-US holidays, places outside the world clock's table, and currency (online, batch 5).

**3. Device settings**: Brightness up, down, set, auto, Ringer mode: vibrate, silent, ring, Airplane mode, Battery saver, Open a settings page, Auto-rotate, Dark mode, Hotspot, Location on or off, NFC and mobile data, Wi-Fi on, off, status, Bluetooth on, off, connect, status, Volume: up, down, set, mute, check, Lock the phone, Screen timeout.

  Gemini's own list of everyday toggles. One skill, one WRITE_SETTINGS grant, the VIS voice activities GAR already uses for DND, and honest settings panels where Android needs system privileges. Settle the device_setting tool merge here, before the FunctionGemma fine-tune.

  Status 2026-10-27: done except lock the phone and 'what's the volume' (DESIGN.md: 'Device settings'). One English skill, device_settings, and one model tool, device_setting{setting, action, level}, which took the wifi and bluetooth tools' calls: 27 tools before and after batch 3's merge and batch 4's media_play. Brightness (along the slider's curve), adaptive brightness, auto-rotate and the screen timeout through WRITE_SETTINGS, asked on a card; the ringer, silent through DND access; airplane mode and battery saver through Settings' voice activities while GAR is the assistant, else their page; hotspot, location, NFC, mobile data and dark mode open their page or panel and say why; status questions for every setting but the hotspot; 'open <page> settings' for 35 pages, and the model's open_app('battery settings') through Kotlin. Still missing: lock the phone (device admin or accessibility: its own decision), 'what's the volume', connecting a Bluetooth device (no public API), the Wi-Fi network's name (needs location), continuing a command once WRITE_SETTINGS is granted, and a check on the Pixel (the voice activities on GrapheneOS 17, the hotspot page, battery saver's first-time dialog). The merged catalog ran in the 2026-10-27 eval (gar/eval/README.md, 'Latest results'): the merge stays. Its device_setting slots weren't reliable, so since the review the app checks each call against the words (a question is the status, another setting named drops the call, a switch the words don't name asks first) and runs the volume calls about the brightness as the brightness (DESIGN.md, 'Device settings'); an eval run after those fixes is still open.

  Weather is a top daily request and today has no forecast and no model route. Translation can become offline through Offline Translator, already on the phone. Everything else follows the online-skills rule (off until a yes).

  Status 2026-11-02: done (DESIGN.md: 'Weather', 'Online answers: knowledge, definitions, currency, translation', 'Online skills', 'Batches 5 and 6 together'). **7. Conversation (phase 3)**: Follow-ups on the last command.

  The biggest UX gap after the overlay; it pairs with the UX items continued conversation, speak vs show and chips, which belong to the conversation and makeover phases in Aaron's order.

  Status 2026-11-02: phase 1 done, with continued conversation and speaking vs showing; no tool, prompt, grammar or golden change (DESIGN.md: 'Conversation memory', 'Continued conversation', 'Speaking vs showing', 'Stop: who takes it'). A first English skill, follow_up, parses whole-input forms in :gar:router (FollowUpForms) and rewrites them from a RAM memory of the last 3 turns (5 minutes, cleared over the lock screen) into the full command, which is matched again through the normal path; it scores 0.94, so every whole sentence and always-best skill keeps its own pronoun phrases. Works: 'turn it (back) off/on' (also 'turn it off again', 'no, turn it back off', a trailing 'thank you'), 'turn it up/down' and 'set it to 50' after the flashlight, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Do Not Disturb, dark mode and the other device settings, brightness and volume (also after 'what's the volume', now a volume sentence); 'cancel it', 'delete it' (within a minute), 'change it to 8' and 'no, 8 pm' on an alarm, keeping its days; cancel, pause, resume, 'add 5 minutes' and 'make it 10' on a timer ('make it 10' within 15 s of setting it adds or takes off the difference: the clock app can't set a running timer's length, open question 1); 'stop it' and 'reset it' on the stopwatch; 'what about tomorrow', 'and in denver', 'no, boston' on the weather, 'and in london' on the time, 'what about easter' on holidays; 'another one' and 'again' after a joke, a coin, dice or a random number; 'call her', 'text her', 'what's her number' after a unique contact, and 'send it to mom too' or 'I said erin' after a text the user sent. When nothing fits GAR asks ('Turn what off?', 'Who should I call?'), and 'turn it off' while music plays pauses it; 'turn it off' never walks back past the music or a clock to an older setting, and 'cancel it' needs the clock to be the latest answer. Continued turns confirm cancelling, deletions and changes first ('Cancel your 7 am alarm?'). Continued listening: a 7 s window after spoken answers of allow-listed skills, Whisper only, with a Whisper-hallucination filter (a bare 'thank you' counts as one), 'thanks' ending it, no window while music plays out loud (Bluetooth speakers and cars included), and an emptying arc on the mic (setting on by default, open question 2). Typed input is answered on the card only unless 'Speak answers to typed requests' is on. Tests: 1236 app unit tests and 234 router tests pass, among them GarFollowUpChatReplayTest's twelve conversations. Still missing: phase 2 (places through Direct coordinates, media and song facts, notes and calendar, units, currency and translation corrections, Direct for two Erins), 'undo that' (open question 5), non-English follow-ups, and the Pixel checks.

**8. Routines and later**: Routines, Several commands in one sentence, Calls and messages through Signal or WhatsApp, Answer, decline or end a call by voice, Location reminders, Jokes and fun answers, News, Sports scores, Travel time and traffic, Go home, go back, recent apps, Power off or restart, What's on my screen, read this page, Find my phone.

  Large effort, rare use, or blocked by Android (power, find my phone, screen understanding). Routines are worth doing once the commands they chain exist.

## Keeping the model's prompt small

Before batch 3: 27 tools, about 2300 prompt tokens (roughly 85 per tool).
